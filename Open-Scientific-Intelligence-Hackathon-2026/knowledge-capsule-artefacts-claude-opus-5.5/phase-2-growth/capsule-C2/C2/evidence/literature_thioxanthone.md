# Literature triplet energy of parent thioxanthone (calibration reference)

Retrieved 2026-10-20.

## Source used (secondary)

Rodrigues, J. F.; Silva, F. A.; Netto-Ferreira, J. C. *Laser flash photolysis study of the photochemistry
of thioxanthone in organic solvents.* J. Braz. Chem. Soc. **2010**, 21(6). doi:10.1590/S0103-50532010000600003
(open access, SciELO: http://www.scielo.br/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0103-50532010000600003).

Quoted excerpts (the fetch tool shortened the first sentence):

- Introduction, about thioxanthone (TX) in hydroxylic solvents: "with a triplet energy located at 65.5 kcal mol-1.5,6"
- Results and Discussion, about the quenching experiments in acetonitrile: "the triplet energy of TX is above 61 kcal mol-1.10,19"

The values are attributed to these references:
- 5. Dalton, J. C.; Turro, N. J. *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **1971**, 93, 6230.
- 6. Dalton, J. C.; Montgomery, F. C. *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **1974**, 96, 6230.
- 10. Lathioor, E. C.; Leigh, W. *Photochem. Photobiol.* **2006**, 82, 291.
- 19. Herkstroeter, W. G.; Lamola, A. A.; Hammond, G. S. *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **1964**, 86, 4537.

Conversion (agent): 65.5 kcal/mol × 4.184 = 274.1 kJ/mol = 2.840 eV.
6a (paper): 269 kJ/mol = 2.788 eV. The difference is 5 kJ/mol (0.05 eV), consistent with the paper's statement that the photophysics of 6a is "comparable to parent 9H-thioxanthen-9-one".
